## Method as implemented

Replay trigger, "synchronous events" (SI Appendix, "Detection of synchronous event", SI p. 7–8):

- Main text: "SWR-associated synchronous spikes of neuronal populations within a short (∼100 ms) time window when animals stopped, termed 'synchronous events' (Fig. 3A and SI Appendix, Fig. S6 A–D)" (Results, p. 3).

- Signal: sorted units. "For each rat, the instantaneous spike rates (bin = 1 ms) were averaged over all the recorded neurons and smoothed with a Gaussian filter (σ = 15 ms)." (114, 72, 68, 79 and 22 cells per rat, SI "Spike sorting".)

- Normalization: "the mean and standard deviation (SD) of instantaneous spike rates during stop periods with a running speed of less than 5 cm/s were computed from all the recorded neurons, termed the meanbase rate and SDbase rate ... Using these baseline variables, the instantaneous spike rates were z-scored".

- Threshold and cells: "Candidate synchronous events were detected when the z-scored spike rates exceeded 2 and the number of active neurons exceeded 4." The window over which "active" is counted is not stated (inference: within the candidate).

- Bounds: "The onset and offset of candidate synchronous events were marked at the time points when the z-scored spike rates first exceeded and fell below 0, respectively."

- Duration: "Candidate synchronous events in which the duration was less than 50 ms or more than 2000 ms were excluded from further analyses." Applies to the 0-crossing bounds.

- Ripple power across tetrodes: "In each tetrode, the LFP signals were bandpass filtered at 150–250 Hz, and the envelope of the filtered LFP traces was computed via the Hilbert transformation. Then, the envelope was smoothed with a Gaussian filter (σ = 4 ms). In addition, baseline mean and SD vectors of the smoothed envelopes during the stop periods were computed in each tetrode, termed meanbase power and SDbase power, respectively. Based on these variables, the Mahalanobis distance was computed at each time point, termed overall ripple power." Only mean and SD vectors are mentioned, not a covariance matrix (inference: diagonal covariance, i.e. the Euclidean norm of per-tetrode z-scores). Fig. S6A: "the corresponding Mahalanobis distance computed from the thirteen 150–250 Hz bandpass-filtered LFP traces".

- GMM selection: "Using the Gaussian mixture model (GMM), the distributions of the z-scored spike rate and z-scored overall ripple power in all candidate synchronous events were each divided into two distributions, as shown in Supplementary Fig. 6D. Synchronous events were detected when time points were included in the larger distributions of z-scored spike rate or z-scored overall ripple power (colored in magenta in Supplementary Fig. 6D)." Fig. S6D legend: "Relationship between the Mahalanobis distance and z-scored spike rates. Synchronous events were defined when the dots were included in the magenta cluster." Ambiguities: 1-D GMMs per variable ("each divided") or one 2-D GMM ("cluster"); "larger" presumably means the higher-valued component; "or" versus the figure's single cluster; how per-time-point membership becomes an event (any sample in the component? the peak?) is not stated.

- Speed: stop periods (< 5 cm/s) define the baseline; the main text places events "when animals stopped". An explicit speed rule on events is not stated in the SI.

- Maximum duration 2000 ms; merging of close events: not stated. No theta or sleep criterion (task sessions).

- Sampling: LFP "sampled at 2 kHz and low-pass filtered at 500 Hz" (SI "Electrophysiological recording").

Separate SWR detection (SWR rates; SI "Detection of SWRs", p. 8):

- "In a tetrode, LFP signals were bandpass filtered at 150–250 Hz, and the envelope of the filtered LFP traces was computed via the Hilbert transformation. The envelope was then smoothed with a Gaussian filter (σ = 4 ms), termed ripple power. In addition, the mean and SD of ripple power during stop periods with a running speed of less than 5 cm in the task periods were computed in each tetrode ... SWR events were detected when the envelope exceeded (meanbase power + 3 × SDbase power). The onset and offset of SWR events were marked at the time points when the ripple power first exceeded and fell below meanbase power, respectively. SWR events with a duration less than 50 ms or more than 500 ms were excluded." Whether this runs on one tetrode or on each with a merge is not stated ("In a tetrode"; Fig. S6A marks "the times at which ripples were detected in individual electrodes").

Online closed-loop detection (disruption experiments only, not replay detection): envelope of 100–400 Hz filtered LFP of one tetrode, running estimate of mean/SD, "SWRs were detected online when the animal's running speed was below 5 cm/s and when the envelope exceeded the detection threshold of 3–4 standard deviations" (SI "Closed-loop electrical stimulation").

Later analysis restrictions (not detection): weighted correlation and sequence score (rZ) classify synchronous events as forward/reverse replays (Results); Bayesian decoding of synchronous events.
