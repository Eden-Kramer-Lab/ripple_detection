## Method as implemented

Detection (population burst events, PBEs):

- Main text: "we identified population burst events (PBEs) based on multiunit activities, which included all putative spikes recorded in the CA1. A PBE was defined as a time window (50–400 ms) with peak multiunit activity ≥4 s.d. above baseline." (Results, p. 573).

- Signal: "PBEs were defined from multiunit activity, which included all putative spikes recorded by all tetrodes in the CA1 on a given day16,17,34." (Online Methods, "Population burst events (PBEs) and LFP analysis"). Spikes were "identified by a threshold of 60 µV" (Online Methods, "Data acquisition"). This is unsorted multiunit from 24 tetrodes (bilateral CA1).

- Binning and smoothing: "Multiunit spikes were counted in each 10-ms time bin." No smoothing is stated.

- Threshold and bounds: "A PBE was defined as a time period of 50–400 ms within which the peak spike count exceeded the mean by at least 4 s.d. The start and end of the PBE were timepoints when the spike count crossed the mean." (Online Methods). So the peak is ≥ mean + 4 SD, the bounds are the mean crossings, and the duration is 50–400 ms, applied to the mean-bounded period.

- Normalization period: not stated. "On a given day" describes the pooled spikes. Whether the mean and SD are taken per session, per day, or during a particular state is not stated.

- Merging of close events: not stated.

- Speed or behaviour: no speed criterion on PBE detection is stated. Speed < 3 cm/s appears elsewhere: "Spikes occurring within population burst events (PBEs ...) and when rats were stationary (speed < 3 cm/s) were excluded" from place-field rate curves (Online Methods, "Place cell quantification"), and "the pausing period as the time window before a turn when the speed was lower than 3 cm/s" (Online Methods, "Behavioral analysis"). Both are analysis definitions.

- Brain state: none for detection. The track sessions are awake. Rest-box sessions exist, but the analysed replay is on-track.

- Participation (analysis, not detection): "we defined a PBE with at least four active template cells as a candidate event" (Online Methods, "Identification of replays and theta sequences"). Template cells are single-field place cells in Pre or re-exposure.

- LFP check (validation, not detection): the ripple band (100–250 Hz) of one pyramidal-layer tetrode was Hilbert transformed, "The absolute values (amplitudes) of the Hilbert transform were smoothed using a Gaussian kernel with a 4-ms s.d. Amplitudes were then normalized by their mean and s.d. as z-scores." This was used to show that PBEs coincide with ripples (Supp. Fig. 4).

- A separate theta/delta ratio > 2 was used only to find theta cycles for theta-sequence analysis ("Identification of theta cycles"). It is not a PBE criterion.

## Inherited from

The paper cites refs 16 (Diba & Buzsáki 2007), 17 (Davidson et al. 2009) and 34 (Ji & Wilson 2007) for the MUA-based PBE idea. The rule itself (10 ms bins, peak ≥ 4 SD, bounds at the mean, 50–400 ms) is stated in full. It differs from Davidson (1 ms bins, σ = 15 ms, 3 SD, STOP statistics) and from Diba (a population-rate increase preceded by ≥ 60 ms of silence, according to a quick look at Diba & Buzsáki 2007, row 51). So I followed no citation further.
