## Method as implemented

- Main text (p. 1241): "we identified reactivation events at both ends of the track by an increase in the population activity during immobility10, when ≥30% or ≥5 of the place cells, whichever was greater, fired in 300-ms windows that were preceded by ≥60-ms silence".

- Supplementary Methods (verbatim): "Pre-play and replay sequences were collected only when the animal was in one of the two track-end reward areas." "When the animal's speed was ≤ 10 cm s–1 (~'immobility'; about 77 percent of the time spent in reward areas), pre-play/replay events were detected by searching for silent periods ≥ 60 ms. If ≥ 5 or ≥ 30 percent of place-cells (whichever was greater) from a template fired within the next 300 ms, an event was recorded."

- Units: "Well-isolated pyramidal cells with stable single place-fields of peak ≥ 5 Hz on the track"; templates per running direction ("average 9.1 members"). Sorted single units, not multiunit.

- Whose silence: not stated (template place cells, all place cells, or all units). Overlap between windows: not stated.

- Event bounds: fixed 300 ms window starting at the end of the silence. No duration or merging rules beyond that.

- Behavioral criteria: speed ≤ 10 cm/s AND location in a reward area (both detection criteria).

- No LFP, theta, or sleep criterion in detection.

- ANALYSIS, not detection: sequence = first spike of each cell in the 300 ms window; rank-order correlation vs 500 identity shuffles.

- Ripples (used only for the event–ripple cross-correlogram, Fig. 2a; Supplementary Methods): "by band-pass filtering the local field potential from a recording site in the CA1 pyramidal layer the root-mean-square power was calculated in the 100-300 Hz ripple-band; ripples were detected when a threshold of 2 standard deviations (SDs) above the mean was exceeded. Onset of ripples was marked at the point when the ripple power was at 1.5 SDs above the mean."

## Inherited from

Main text cites Foster & Wilson 2006 (ref. 10) for "an increase in the population activity during immobility" — the silence/300 ms rule itself is fully given in the Supplementary Methods, so not followed. Ripple detection "was previously described (Csicsvari et al., 1999b)": J Neurosci 19:RC20 (a local copy, buzsakilab.com PDF) — "digitally bandpass-filtered (80–250 Hz). The power (root mean square) of the filtered signal was calculated for each electrode and summed across electrodes ... summing squared sample values within a 1.6 msec window ... Oscillatory epochs with a power of ≥2 SD above the mean were detected. The beginning and the end of oscillatory epochs were marked at points at which the power reduced to <1 SD. Theta periods ... were excluded". Diba changes band (100–300), channel (one site) and onset (1.5 SD).
