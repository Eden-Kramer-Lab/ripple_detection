## Method as implemented

DETECTION (STAR Methods, "Sharp-wave ripple detection", p. e3):

- "SWRs were detected as described previously during immobility periods (≤4 cm/s) (Jadhav et al., 2016; Karlsson and Frank, 2009; Tang et al., 2017). In brief, LFPs from CA1 tetrodes were filtered into the ripple band (150-250 Hz), and the envelope of the ripple-filtered LFPs was determined using a Hilbert transform."

- Threshold and combination: "SWRs were initially detected as contiguous periods when the envelope stayed above 3 SD of the mean on at least one tetrode" → any-tetrode (Karlsson rule).

- Bounds: "and further refined as times around the initially detected events during which the envelope exceeded the mean."

- Immobility definition (Behavioral state definition): "immobility was defined as periods with speed ≤ 4 cm/s". Detection is restricted to immobility periods; which samples of an event are tested is not stated.

- Smoothing: not stated. Minimum duration of the above-threshold run: not stated. Normalization period: not stated. Maximum duration: not stated. Overlap merging: not stated (inherited).

- LFPs "sampled at 1.5 kHz and bandpass filtered between 0.5 Hz and 400 Hz" (p. e2).

ANALYSIS restrictions:

- Duration (p. e3): "For replay and reactivation analysis (see below, Replay Prediction and CA1-PFC Population Reactivation Analysis), only SWRs with a duration ≥ 50 ms were included, similar to previous studies (Pfeiffer and Foster, 2013; Wu et al., 2017)." Duration = whole event (onset to offset after extension to the mean), and it applies only to replay/reactivation analyses, not to SWR detection (SWR counts/durations in Fig. 4F use all SWRs).

- Replay candidates (Replay detection): "candidate events were defined as the SWR events during which ≥ 5 place cells fired" (10 ms bins).

- Reactivation candidates: "SWR events during which ≥ 5 place cells and ≥ 5 PFC cells fired".

- Engaged/disengaged split of well immobility periods (behavioral analysis only).

## Inherited from

Jadhav 2016 / Karlsson & Frank 2009 / Tang 2017 (see those findings): 4 ms Gaussian smoothing and >= 15 ms above 3 SD on at least one tetrode (Karlsson), bounds at mean, overlapping events combined. Inference: the unstated smoothing and minimum run are these inherited values.

  - `minstd = 3`, `minrip = 1`, `velfilter = 4` (lines 6-9); `inripple = (nrip >= minrip)` (line 117): an event on any one ripple tetrode.
  - Each tetrode's events are 15 ms above 3 SD ([JadhavLab/Jadhav-Lab-Codes-JHB @973932a](https://github.com/JadhavLab/Jadhav-Lab-Codes-JHB/tree/973932a), `BetaOdorProject/figures1-6Functions/OLD/Archive 20200206/Ripples/cs_extractRipples.m` lines 4-5); `cs_rippletimes.m` there counts tetrodes the same way (lines 12, 51).

- **Rules in the code, not in the paper:** joined events of 50 ms or less are dropped for all SWRs (line 141), whereas the paper applies >= 50 ms to replay candidates only; speed is tested at the event's midpoint (line 92), not its start and end.

- `DFTFsj_getriptimes` itself is not public; DANDI 000978 has no ripple table.

## Analysis and interpretation

The approximate 40% replay fraction summarizes the session means in Figure S5E; individual animals and sessions vary.

## Uncertainties

Related Jadhav-lab code permits caller overrides and does not establish Shin’s paper-specific invocation. Its midpoint speed and whole-SWR duration tests must not silently replace the publication’s replay criteria.
