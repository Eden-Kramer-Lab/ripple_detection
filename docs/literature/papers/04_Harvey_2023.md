## Method as implemented

STAR Methods, "SWR detection" (p. e5), verbatim:

- "To detect SWRs, the wide-band signal was band-pass filtered (difference-of-Gaussians; zero-lag, linear phase FIR), and instantaneous power was calculated by clipping at 4 SD, rectified, and low-pass filtered.32 The low-pass filter cut-off was at a frequency corresponding to p cycles of the mean bandpass (for 80-250 Hz band-pass, the low-pass was 55 Hz)."

- "Subsequently, the power of the non-clipped signal was computed, and all events exceeding 4 SD from the mean were detected. The events were then expanded until the (non-clipped) power fell below 1 SD; short events (< 15 ms) were discarded."

- "Sharp waves were detected separately using LFP from a CA1 str. radiatum channel, filtered with band-pass filter boundaries (5-40 Hz). LFP events of a minimum duration of 20 ms and a maximum of 400 ms exceeding 2.5 SD of the background signal were included as candidate SPWs. Only if a sharp wave was simultaneously detected with a ripple was a CA1 SPW-R event retained for further analysis."

- Not stated here: number of ripple channels, period for mean/SD, SW polarity, merging, state or speed restriction for detection. LFP at "1,250-Hz down-sampled signal".

- State scoring ("Detection of brain states"): buzcode SleepScoreMaster (Levenstein et al. 2019) — broadband-LFP PC1, theta ratio 5–10 / 2–16 Hz, EMG from 300–600 Hz inter-site correlation, "Soft sticky thresholds". Fully automatic as described.

- ANALYSIS, not detection (replay): "Replay events were identified from events with at least 5 active pyramidal cells with a peak firing rate of ≥1Hz and a peak-to-mean firing rate ratio of 1. In addition, candidate events with many (> 50%) inactive spike count bins were discarded." 20 ms bins. The 4 cm/s speed appears only for place fields and a movement control.

## Inherited from

- Ref. 32 = Oliva et al. 2016. Supplemental Experimental Procedures: same DOG/clipping/4 SD algorithm "(Stark et al., 2014)", run "independently on each recording site", with "The mean and SD of baseline LFP were computed based on the power during non-REM sleep", bounds at "below 2 SD" (Harvey says 1 SD), "short events (<15 ms) were discarded, and adjacent events (gap <15 ms) were merged", SW on "str. radiatum channels ... (5-40 Hz) adjusted to match the features of sharp-waves ... minimum duration of 20 ms and maximum 400 ms exceeding 2.5 SD". State: theta ratio 5–11 Hz vs 1–4 and 12–14 Hz "followed by manual adjustment"; WAKE = low theta and speed < 2 cm/s.

- Fernández-Ruiz et al. 2019 (Harvey's text is copied from it): adds "The mean and SD of baseline LFP were computed based on the power during non-REM sleep" and "SPW-Rs were detected only during immobility and low-theta power periods. Immobility periods were defined as periods when the detected speed was < 5 cm/s for at least 1s." Harvey omits both sentences, so whether they applied is unknown.

## Code

- Paper: "Original code has been deposited at https://github.com/ayalab1 and https://github.com/ryanharvey1/ripple_heterogeneity"; neurocode Zenodo badge 398122107.

- What the repository actually calls (CODE CONTRADICTS THE TEXT; it is a multi-dataset pipeline with per-dataset detectors):
  - `ripple_heterogeneity/matlab/update_session_data.m`: `ripples = DetectSWR(channels,...)` with `channels = [swrCh.ripple swrCh.sharpwave]`, then `eventSpikingTreshold(ripples,'spikes',spikes,...,'spikingThreshold',0.5)`.
  - `formatting_data/custom_aya/convert_aya10_day12.m`: `DetectSWR([54 60],'saveMat',true)`; `custom_or/OR_other.m` and `custom_fujisawaS/custom_for_fujisawaS.m`: `DetectSWR(... 'thresSDswD',[0.25, 1],'thresSDrip',[0.25, 1],'forceDetect',true,'check',true)` (manual curation; `check` forces `useSPW=false` in DetectSWR).
  - `formatting_data/custom_kenji/run_ripple_pipe_kenji.m` (CRCNS hc-3) and `custom_girardeau/run_ripple_pipe_girardeau.m`: `bz_FindRipples`/`FindRipples(... 'durations',[50,300], 'thresholds',[1,3], 'passband',[100,250], 'minDuration',20, 'EMGFromLFP',...)` on the channel with the highest 100–250 Hz power ratio — NO radiatum channel — then `eventSpikingTreshold`.
  - `matlab/SWRpipeline.m`: `bz_DetectSWR` if a sharp-wave channel exists, else `bz_FindRipples`.
  - `make_ripple_stats_all_sessions.m` branches on `detectorname` containing `bz_DetectSWR` vs FindRipples, i.e. sessions with both kinds were pooled.

- neurocode `eventSpikingTreshold.m`: CA1 spikes binned (`bz_SpktToSpkmat`, dt 10 ms, overlap 6), population count per unit z-scored over the session; keep an event if the mean z within ±10 ms (`eventSize` 0.01) of its peak exceeds 0.5 SD.

- Which script produced the events in the paper's figures cannot be told from the repository; the paper uses in-house Cornell data plus CRCNS hc-3, pfc-2, hc-11, hc-14 (key resources table), which is consistent with the mixed pipeline.

### Related code

Commits are the last before publication (5 July 2023); the cited lines are unchanged at `main` (bcaf142).

- **Own.** [ryanharvey1/ripple_heterogeneity @8461a94](https://github.com/ryanharvey1/ripple_heterogeneity/tree/8461a94), `ripple_heterogeneity/replay/replay_run.py`, which `main_loop` runs with its defaults:
  - line 431, `speed_thres=4,  # running threshold to determine tuning curves`, used only in `get_tuning_curves` (line 323). The 4 cm/s is not an event criterion.
  - line 432, `min_rip_dur=0.08,  # min ripple duration for replay`, applied at line 408: replay candidates are ripples of at least 80 ms. The paper does not state this; it gives only the ripple detector's 15 ms.
  - line 597, `idx = (n_active >= 5) & (inactive_bin_prop < 0.5)`: at least 5 active place cells and fewer than 50% empty 20 ms bins. This differs at exactly 50%: the paper discards only events with more than 50% empty bins, whereas the code also rejects equality.
  - line 427, `traj_shuff=1500,  # number of shuffles to determine sig replay`, passed as `n_shuffles` at line 633: 1500 shuffles per candidate. The text says 1000 surrogates.
  - Candidate ripples are not restricted by speed or state; overlapping ones are merged.

- **Own.** [ryanharvey1/ripple_heterogeneity @7b368a9](https://github.com/ryanharvey1/ripple_heterogeneity/tree/7b368a9), `ripple_heterogeneity/matlab/update_session_data.m` lines 67, 73 and 76: `channels = [swrCh.ripple swrCh.sharpwave]`, then `DetectSWR(channels,'saveMat',true,'basepath',basepath)` with no threshold options. So one ripple channel and neurocode's defaults ([ayalab1/neurocode v1.0.0 @4b33b2a](https://github.com/ayalab1/neurocode/tree/4b33b2a), `SharpWaveRipples/DetectSWR.m` lines 184-195): sharp wave 2-50 Hz, ripple 80-250 Hz, 0.5 and 2.5 local SD for both, sharp wave 20-500 ms, ripple at least 25 ms, no ripple maximum. The repository does not pin a neurocode version; buzcode's 2015 `detect_swr.m` has the same defaults.

- **Own.** Sessions without a radiatum channel: `ripple_heterogeneity/formatting_data/custom_kenji/run_ripple_pipe_kenji.m` lines 85-87 and `custom_girardeau/run_ripple_pipe_girardeau.m` lines 62-64 (@7b368a9) call FindRipples with `'durations',[50,300]`, `'thresholds',[1,3]`, `'passband',[100,250]`. The code does not record which sessions reached the replay analysis.

- **Divergence from the text.** The text describes difference-of-Gaussians ripple power (4 SD, extended to 1 SD, at least 15 ms) and a 2.5 SD, 20-400 ms sharp wave; the code runs DetectSWR. The text's 1000 replay surrogates are 1500 in `run_all`. The CSV records the released implementation and preserves the published/code differences in its notes.

## Uncertainties

The dependency versions and every dataset/session caller are not pinned. Explicit FindRipples arguments are known where present; shared DetectSWR defaults do not prove every historical session setting. Manual curation is not reproducible from the release alone.

## Package mapping

Packaged DetectSWR path: `harvey_2023_code`; published-text path: `harvey_2023_text`; FindRipples branch without radiatum: `harvey_2023_no_radiatum` in [literature_methods.py](../../../src/ripple_detection/literature_methods.py). Its docstring records implementation choices and assumptions. Simulation checks establish that it runs; they do not establish equivalence to the authors’ original event set.

Each path defaults to detection. `stage="decoding_candidates"` adds the released replay selection: >=80 ms before merging overlapping ripples, >=5 selected place cells and fewer than 50% empty 20 ms bins. Place-cell selection and EMG/manual curation are caller responsibilities.
