# Yang 2024 — Selection of experience for memory by hippocampal sharp wave ripples
Source: the extracted text (main text + Supplementary Materials, Dropbox PDF); title verified: yes
Also consulted: Huszár et al. 2022 Nat Neurosci (source of dataset 1, DANDI 000552) via Wayback copy of PMC10807234 (a local copy); neurocode `find_HSE.m` and `find_rippleHSE.m` (github.com/ayalab1/neurocode, SharpWaveRipples/); the event file Data/e13_26m1_210913.rippleHSE.events.mat in the paper's analysis repo.
Trigger: SWR+MUA (Grosmark 2016 conjunction: population synchrony event containing an LFP ripple)

## Method as implemented
Supplementary Materials, "Ripple detection" (p. 4) — near-verbatim copy of Grosmark & Buzsáki 2016:
- "Ripple events were conditioned on the coincidence of both population synchrony events, and LFP detected ripples as described in ref. (17). For each session the combined spiking of all recorded CA1 pyramidal cells was binned in 1-ms bins and convolved with a 15-ms Gaussian kernel. For each session, a trigger rate was defined as being 3 standard deviations above the mean of all 1-ms bins within NREM epochs of both PRE and POST epochs combined. Putative population synchrony events were detected when the smoothed firing rate vector crossed the trigger rate. The beginning and end of the putative synchrony events were defined as the time points at which the convolved firing rate vector returned to the mean of all within-NREM firing rate bins."
- LFP: "Independently, ripple events were detected from the pyramidal layer LFP." Method NOT STATED.
- "Population synchrony events that (1) contained at least one LFP-detected sharp wave-ripple, (2) lasted between 50 to 500-ms, and (3) occurred during non-theta or 'off-line' states (quite awake immobility or NREM) and 4) in which at least five distinct pyramidal cells each fired at least one spike were termed 'Ripple events'".
- State scoring ("State scoring", p. 3): buzcode SleepScoreMaster — "Spectrograms were computed from broadband LFP with fast-Fourier transform in 10-s sliding windows (at 1-s) and principal component analysis (PCA) was computed after a z transform ... Theta dominance was quantified as the ratio of powers in the 5 to 10-Hz and 2 to 16-Hz frequency bands. EMG was estimated as the zero-lag correlation between 300 and 600-Hz filtered signals across recording sites. Soft sticky thresholds on these metrics were used to identify states ... All assignments were inspected visually and manually curated wherever appropriate" (TheStateEditor).
- No speed number for events ("quiet awake immobility").
- Datasets: (1) mice, DANDI 000552 (Huszár et al. 2022); (2) rats, Grosmark's "Achilles" session; (3) new mice.
- ANALYSIS, not detection: candidate events binned at 20 ms for UMAP/manifold replay and Bayesian decoding.

## Inherited from
- Ref. (17) = Grosmark & Buzsáki 2016 (manifest 36_Grosmark_2016): identical text; also leaves the LFP ripple method unstated.
- Dataset 1's source paper, Huszár et al. 2022 (a Yang co-author's paper; not cited by Yang for ripple detection): "SPW-Rs were detected ... from manually selected channels located in the center of the CA1 pyramidal layer (bz_FindRipples.m). Broadband LFP was bandpass filtered between 130 and 200 Hz using a fourth-order Chebyshev filter, and the normalized squared signal was calculated. SPW-R maxima were detected by thresholding the normalized squared signal at 5 s.d. above the mean, and the surrounding SPW-R start–stop times were identified as crossings of 2 s.d. around this peak. SPW-R duration limits were set to be between 20 and 200 ms." plus a noise-channel veto. This is the likeliest LFP detector for dataset 1 (inference, two hops).

## Code
- Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.10685490 (github.com/winnieyangwannan/Selection-of-experience-for-memory-by-hippocampal-sharp-wave-ripples): Python analysis only; ships Data/e13_26m1_210913.rippleHSE.events.mat with `detectorinfo.detectorname = 'find_HSE'`, `detectionparms = []`, and a `winSize = 0.5` field.
- Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.10685428 (winnieyangwannan/buzcode fork): stock buzcode with both `bz_FindRipples.m` and `detect_swr/detect_swr.m`; no script shows which was called.
- neurocode `find_rippleHSE.m` ("Winnie Yang, Jan 2021, Buzsaki Lab") keeps HSEs via `InIntervals(ripples.timestamps, HSE.timestamps)`; FMAToolbox `InIntervals` tests `values(:,1)`, i.e. the ripple START time, not the peak.
- neurocode `find_HSE.m` (Hainmueller 2020), 2021 version: 1 ms bins, Gaussian SD = `tSmooth` 15 ms (`fspecial('gauss',[10*fsize 1],fsize)`), `zscore` over ALL bins of the recording (not NREM-only), threshold `> 3`, bounds where the z-scored trace drops below `spkmean` (the mean raw count per 1 ms bin, compared against z-scores — a units bug; fixed to 0 in the 2022 revision), `evtdur > 0.05 & evtdur < 0.5`. No state or cell-count criterion inside.
- Code-vs-text discrepancies (the version Yang ran is inferred from the empty `detectionparms`, which matches the 2021 version, and the extra `winSize` field implies a local edit): normalization over the whole recording vs "within NREM"; containment of the ripple start vs "peak ripple power"; and the shipped file has 73 of 4266 events shorter than 50 ms (min 37 ms), inconsistent with "lasted between 50 to 500-ms" unless filtered later.

## Survey CSV discrepancies
- SWR Z-score Thresh. = 3: not stated for the LFP ripple (3 SD is the MUA trigger; Huszár's bz_FindRipples used 5 SD peak / 2 SD bounds).
- SWR Low/High Band = 150/250: not stated (Huszár: 130–200 Hz).
- SWR electrodes = 1: not stated.
- Detection Notes "during NREM (manually scored)": events come from NREM and quiet waking; state scoring is automatic (SleepScoreMaster) with manual curation.
- MUA 3 SD, 15 ms, Min cells 5, 50–500 ms: agree with the text.

Remaining deviations:

- NREM-only statistics (text) vs whole-recording z-score (code); package can do either via `normalization_mask`.
- 2021 find_HSE bound at z < mean raw count per bin rather than z < 0 (package: z = 0).
- Gaussian SD 15 ms (code confirms SD) with 5-SD truncation (package 8 SD).
