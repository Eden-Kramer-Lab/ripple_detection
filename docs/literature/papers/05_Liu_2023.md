## Method as implemented
Materials and Methods, "SWR detection", verbatim:
- "one channel around the CA1 pyramidal layer was chosen for the ripple detection, and one channel from CA1 stratum radiatum was chosen for sharp wave detection. The difference between the two channels was used as the basis for SWR detection. This difference signal was filtered with a low-pass filter at 55 Hz, and then local minima were detected as candidate events. The corresponding ripple power (amplitude of the difference signal in the bandpass 80 to 250 Hz) for each candidate event was recorded. A true event was considered when both a sharp wave and ripple oscillation were detected in the same window. K-means clustering was used to define clustering of SWR from non-SWR events, and manual curation was used to better define the boundary and remove outliers. The events were then expanded until the (nonclipped) ripple power fell below 1 SD; short events (<15 ms) and longer events (>400 ms) were discarded."
- Population-burst refinement (same section): "To refine the detection of SWR start and end points for reactivation and replay analyses (in order to avoid including empty bins), we detected population burst events as periods when the instantaneous population firing rate (binned in 1-ms bins and smoothed with a Gaussian kernel with 10-ms width) reached a peak of >2 SD and remained greater than the mean. The SWR start and end time points were set as the start and end time points of the population burst within the respective SWR, and SWRs without population bursts were discarded." Which units form the population: not stated here (pyramidal cells per the replay section). "10-ms width": SD or full width not stated.
- Replay candidates ("Candidate replay events"): "periods of elevated pyramidal cell population activity ... when the instantaneous firing rate (1-ms bins, smoothed with a Gaussian kernel of width 10 ms) was greater than the mean and reached a peak of >2 SD above the mean during the session. Candidate events were between 100 and 500 ms in duration, and they each overlapped with an independently detected SWR; otherwise they were excluded". Normalization over the whole session.
- State: SleepScoreMaster-style scoring ("Detection of brain states"; PC1, theta 5–10 / 2–16 Hz, EMG, "Soft sticky thresholds"). SWR analyses are of "pre- and post-task sleep" home-cage sessions; an explicit NREM restriction on SWR detection is not stated.
- Speed: none stated for SWRs ("only periods in which the animals run faster than 5 cm/s" is for active-behavior analyses; >4 cm/s for place fields).
- ANALYSIS, not detection: replay decoded in 20 ms bins, line-fit score with 500 column shuffles; reactivation/EV across SWRs.

## Code
"Custom scripts used in this study can be downloaded from Zenodo (114)" = ayalab1/neurocode Release v1.0.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.7819979 — the same release the package's `Long_sharp_wave_ripple_detector` cites. neurocode `DetectSWR` defaults there: `swBP [2 50]`, `ripBP [80 250]`, `per_thresswD 10`, `per_thresRip 50`, `WinSize 40` ms, `Ns_chk 5` s, `thresSDswD`/`thresSDrip [0.5 2.5]`, `minIsi 0.05`, `minDurSW 0.02`, `maxDurSW 0.5`, `minDurRP 0.025`; `check` (manual curation) sets `useSPW=false`, which drops the local sharp-wave magnitude test and the SW maximum duration and makes bounds the union of SW and ripple intervals. `useSPW=false` still needs the radiatum channel (candidates and k-means use the SW difference). The paper-stated 1 SD / 15 ms / 400 ms bounds do not match these defaults; whether non-default arguments were passed is not recorded in the paper.

Remaining deviations:
- Package Long detector follows DetectSWR defaults (SW-defined bounds at local median + 0.5 SD, SW 20–500 ms, ripple ≥ 25 ms), not the paper's "ripple power < 1 SD, 15–400 ms"; the latter can only be approximated by post-filtering `duration` (the bound rule itself is not a parameter).
- Manual curation of the k-means boundary (`check=True`) and its `useSPW=false` behaviour are not available.
- "Local minima of the 55 Hz low-passed difference" (paper) vs max SW difference in 40 ms blocks after a 2–50 Hz DOG band-pass (DetectSWR/package); sign convention differs only.
- (a) "the population burst within the respective SWR": `require_overlap` keeps bursts that overlap, not only those inside; if several bursts fall in one SWR the paper's choice is unstated.
- Gaussian "width" 10 ms: SD vs FWHM ambiguity.
Smallest package addition (if C): n/a. Without a radiatum channel, what remains implementable is the population-burst part (2 SD peak, mean bounds, 100–500 ms); the SWR gate would have to be replaced by a single-channel ripple detector (e.g. `Kay_ripple_detector` or `Zugaro_ripple_detector` on 80–250 Hz), which is a substitution, not Liu's method.
