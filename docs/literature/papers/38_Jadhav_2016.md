## Method as implemented

DETECTION:

- Main text, Experimental Procedures, "SWR Detection and Modulation": "SWRs were detected using LFPs filtered in the 150–250 Hz range on multiple CA1 tetrodes as previously described (Karlsson and Frank, 2009)."

- Supplemental Experimental Procedures, "SWR Detection": "SWRs were detected using LFPs filtered in the 150-250 Hz range on multiple CA1 tetrodes as previously described (Karlsson and Frank, 2009). Increases in power in the ripple band were detected using a 3 s.d. criterion. A speed criterion of < 4 cm/sec was also applied to avoid any spurious detection of high frequency fast gamma episodes which occur during running (Kemere et al., 2013)."

- Fig. 2 legend: "The threshold speed, 4 cm/s, used to detect SWRs is indicated on the speed plot." → speed is part of detection here. Which sample(s) are tested: not stated.

- Channels: "LFP, filtered 0.5-400 Hz and sampled at 1500 Hz) from all tetrodes (one channel was chosen from each tetrode for LFP recording)" (Supplemental, recordings). CA1 tetrodes only for SWRs.

- Envelope, smoothing, minimum duration, bounds, normalization period, combination rule: not restated; deferred to Karlsson & Frank 2009 (Hilbert envelope, 4 ms Gaussian, >= 15 ms above 3 s.d. on at least one tetrode, extended to the mean, overlapping events combined).

- Close events (Supplemental, SWR Detection): "we discarded all SWRs that occurred within 1 sec of a previously detected SWR, ensuring unambiguous assignment of PFC spikes with respect to SWR onset time." Stated as part of the SWR set used throughout (19,888 SWRs reported after it), so treated here as a detection-stage rule (drop, not merge).

- Maximum duration: not stated. Observed mean duration 96.7 +/- 1.2 ms.

ANALYSIS restrictions:

- Replay analysis (Supplemental): "only SWRs with at-least 4 CA1 cells spiking during SWRs were considered candidate events" (15 ms bins).

- Theta periods (Supplemental): "speed criterion of >5 cm/sec and no SWRs detected on any of the CA1 tetrodes with a 3 s.d. criterion" — confirms per-tetrode (any-tetrode) SWR detection.

## Inherited from

Karlsson & Frank 2009 ([49_Karlsson_2009.md](49_Karlsson_2009.md)): one channel per tetrode, 150–250 Hz, Hilbert envelope, 4 ms Gaussian, >= 15 ms above 3 s.d. on at least one tetrode, bounds at the mean, overlapping events combined across tetrodes. Jadhav 2016 differs from Karlsson 2009 by using CA1 tetrodes only (Karlsson used CA3 + CA1) and 4 cm/s rather than 2 cm/s.
