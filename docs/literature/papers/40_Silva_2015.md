# Silva 2015 — Trajectory events across hippocampal place cells require previous experience
Source: the extracted text (pdftotext of the Dropbox PDF, including the Online Methods); title verified: yes (Silva, Feng & Foster, Nat Neurosci 18(12), 2015)
Trigger: MUA (the spike density of sorted units or place cells, not threshold-crossing multiunit)

## Method as implemented

Detection (candidate population events). Quotes are from Online Methods, "Place cell ensemble activity analysis", unless noted:
- Speed and state: "Place cell ensemble activity was examined during stopping periods where the speed of the animal was less than 5 cm/s." Results: "Candidate population events were identified as periods of increased spike density across all recorded units (Fig. 1c), restricted to times when the animal's speed was below 5 cm/s (see Online Methods)."
- Signal and unit set: sorted units. Results says "all recorded units", while the Fig. 1c legend says "spike density (middle) of 114 simultaneously recorded place cells". The text leaves open whether all sorted units or only place cells were used. Units were sorted manually (XCLUST). "Putative inhibitory neurons were excluded on the basis of spike width. Well-isolated clusters were confirmed as those with an L-ratio < 0.05" (Online Methods, "Data acquisition").
- Bins: not stated.
- Smoothing, threshold and bounds: "Candidate population events were identified during periods of increased spike density with peaks >3 s.d. above the mean in the smoothed histogram (Gaussian kernel, s.d. of 10 ms). Start and end boundaries for each candidate population event were defined where the smoothed histogram crossed the mean."
- Normalization period: not stated explicitly. Inference from "examined during stopping periods": the mean and SD come from stopping periods (<5 cm/s), as in Pfeiffer & Foster 2013.
- Duration: "Only candidate events with 100 to 500 ms duration were included in the study." This applies to the mean-crossing-bounded event.
- Merging of close events: not stated. No participation minimum in the detection rule.
- Brain state: candidates are detected identically in run sessions and in the "sleep" sessions, where rats "slept or remained at quiet rest" (Online Methods, "Behavior and pharmacology"). Speed below 5 cm/s is the only criterion. There is no sleep scoring, theta or delta criterion.

Later analysis restrictions (not detection):
- "A small portion of candidate events (15 of 9,803 in sleep 1) was excluded because the place cells that emitted spikes during those events had the same peak firing positions" (spike-correlation analysis only).
- Trajectory events are defined from decoding with 20 ms bins: weighted correlation and maximum jump distance thresholds, replay score and sharpness (Results and Online Methods).

## Inherited from
The paper does not cite a source for the candidate-event rule. Decoding is "as previously described24" (Davidson et al. 2009), and tetrode placement is "as previously described5". Inference: the rule is Pfeiffer & Foster 2013's (ref. 6), with the same 10 ms Gaussian, 3 SD, mean-crossing bounds and <5 cm/s restriction, but with a 100–500 ms window in place of 50 ms–2 s and no 10%-participation rule. Pfeiffer 2013 specifies 1 ms bins of clustered units. Silva states no bin size.

```python
from ripple_detection import multiunit_HSE_detector
# units: (n_time, n_units) spike counts per bin of sorted (place) units; bin size unstated, 1 ms assumed (Pfeiffer 2013)
fs = 1000
cand = multiunit_HSE_detector(
    time, units, speed, fs,
    smoothing_sigma=0.010, zscore_threshold=3.0, minimum_duration=0.0,  # peak > 3 SD, bounds at mean
    normalization_mask=speed < 5.0,                                     # inferred: stats from stopping periods
    speed_threshold=5.0,
    maximum_duration=0.5,
)
cand = cand[cand.duration >= 0.100]
```

Remaining deviations:
- Speed: "restricted to times when the animal's speed was below 5 cm/s" is closer to masking out moving samples than to the package's endpoint rule. `units[speed >= 5] = np.nan` with `speed_threshold=np.inf` implements the masking reading.
- The normalization period is inferred, not stated.
- The bin size is not stated (1 ms assumed).
- The unit set is ambiguous (all sorted units vs place cells).
- Threshold inequality: ">3 s.d." (strict) versus ≥. Bounds at z ≥ 0.
- The minimum duration applies to the extended event, so it is applied as a filter on `duration` with `minimum_duration=0.0`. The maximum is handled by `maximum_duration=0.5` on the reported event, which matches.
