Source: the extracted text (pdftotext of the Dropbox PDF, including Online Methods); title verified: yes (Bendor & Wilson, Nat. Neurosci. 15:1439–1444)
Trigger: MUA

## Method as implemented

Detection (reactivation events):
- Main text: "Reactivation events (Fig. 2c) were detected from large transient increases in multiunit activity (MUA)17" (Results, p. 1439). Ref. 17 is Davidson, Kloosterman & Wilson 2009.
- Signal, binning, smoothing: "MUA was measured using a smoothed histogram (1-ms bins, Gaussian kernel, σ = 15 ms) to calculate the firing rate of all units (not necessarily isolated) with spikes that had a peak amplitude greater than 100 µV, across all tetrodes." (Online Methods, "Reactivation event detection"). This is threshold-crossing multiunit, not sorted units.
- Threshold and bounds (two thresholds): "Candidate reactivation events had a minimum z score of 2 throughout the duration of the event, and a minimum peak z score of 4." (same section). So the bounds are the z = 2 crossings, not the mean, and the peak must be ≥ 4.
- Normalization period: not stated. It is a z score, but over which samples or state is not stated.
- Merging: "Multiple events less than 50 ms apart were grouped together into a single event." (same section).
- Minimum duration: "All reactivation events had to be at least 50 ms in duration." (same section). Placed after the grouping sentence, so it presumably applies to the final grouped event (inference from order).
- Maximum duration: not stated.
- Cell participation: none for detection. Neurons "unresponsive during all analyzed reactivation events were excluded" from the rate-bias analysis (analysis).
- Speed: no speed criterion on events. Movement enters only through sleep classification (below).
- Rationale given: "MUA was highly correlated with ripple power but could be measured with greater temporal precision17."

Brain-state classification (used to label events as NREM, awake or REM; the main results are "non-REM sleep replay events"):
- "Non-REM sleep was defined by time periods with high frame activity (upper quartile)10 containing intermittent high MUA (z score of 4 or greater) and at least 25% of places cells active in a 2-s window, and minimal movement (lower quartile) detected using video tracking. REM sleep was defined by the ratio of the spectral power density in the theta band (6–10 Hz) to the overall power being greater than 0.4 (ref. 34). We obtained similar REM sleep detection using an alternative REM sleep detection algorithm (theta/delta ratio > 6)35." (Online Methods, "Classification of sleep"). Ref. 10 is Ji & Wilson 2007 (frames).
- Awake events: "awake reactivation events" were those that "occurred while the rat was awake in its sleep box" (Results, around Fig. 5). The awake criterion is presumably "not NREM, not REM". It is not spelled out.

Analysis restrictions (not detection):
- Sessions were analysed only with "at least 30 reactivation events during awake periods and sleep periods for each acoustic stimulus" (Online Methods).
- Events were assigned to a sound if they occurred "after the onset of sound R (for up to 10.8 s or the onset of the next sound)".
- Decoding used 10 ms windows, and place cells with peak > 0.5 spikes/s and mean < 5 spikes/s.

## Inherited from
- Ref. 17 (Davidson et al. 2009, manifest 50_Davidson_2009) for the MUA approach: 1 ms bins, σ = 15 ms, > 100 µV spikes on all tetrodes. Bendor changes the selection rule. Davidson uses bounds at the mean, peak ≥ 3 SD and statistics over STOP. Bendor uses bounds at z ≥ 2, peak ≥ 4, merging < 50 ms and a minimum of 50 ms. The paper states its own rule in full, so the citation only supplies the signal definition.
- The sleep classification cites Ji & Wilson 2007 for frame activity (manifest 52_Ji_2007). How "frame activity" is quantified in quartiles is not stated here.

## Package mapping
Tier: B     Needs radiatum: n   Needs theta: y (REM: 6–10 Hz / total power > 0.4)   Needs sleep scoring: y (to label NREM vs awake vs REM events; the detection itself runs on the whole rest session)
Recipe:
```python
import numpy as np
from ripple_detection import get_multiunit_population_firing_rate, normalize_signal, merge_close_events, require_overlap
from ripple_detection.core import extend_threshold_to_mean

# multiunit: (n_time, n_tetrodes) counts per 1 ms bin of all spikes > 100 uV (sorted or not)
fs = 1000
rate = get_multiunit_population_firing_rate(multiunit, fs, smoothing_sigma=0.015)
z = normalize_signal(rate)                                   # statistics period not stated: whole session assumed
events = np.asarray(extend_threshold_to_mean(z >= 2, z >= 4, time, minimum_duration=0.0))
events = merge_close_events(events, close_event_threshold=0.050)
events = events[(events[:, 1] - events[:, 0]) >= 0.050]
nrem_events = require_overlap(events, nrem_intervals)       # user-computed sleep classification
```
Smoke-tested on simulate_multiunit output (it runs; 15–18 events on 23 simulated bursts).
Remaining deviations:
- multiunit_HSE_detector cannot do this directly. It extends to the mean, and Bendor bounds at z = 2. extend_threshold_to_mean with two masks gives the rule exactly, but the output is bare intervals without the detector's statistics columns.
- Order: merging is done after the bounds are set and before the 50 ms minimum, as the text orders it.
- merge_close_events joins gaps strictly < 50 ms ("less than 50 ms apart"), which matches.
- The normalization period is not stated. normalize_signal over all samples is an assumption. normalization_mask could restrict it to sleep.
- Event bounds are the first and last sample with z ≥ 2 (1 ms resolution). That is negligible.
- Sleep classification is entirely user code and partly circular: NREM is defined partly by high MUA z ≥ 4 and by place-cell participation in 2 s windows. It also needs "frame activity" à la Ji 2007 and video movement quartiles, and REM needs theta/total power. The package has none of these.
- Assigning an event to a state (overlap vs containment) is not stated. require_overlap is one choice.
Smallest package addition (if C): not needed for B. A `bounds_zscore` (low threshold) option on `multiunit_HSE_detector` would make the detection A. Zugaro and Carey already have two-threshold logic.
