## Method as implemented

Event windows (Supplemental Experimental Procedures, "Sequence detection", PDF p. 21):

- "Stepping through each session in 10 ms steps, the sequence score of place cell activity within a flexible time window (40 ms to 600 ms, to accommodate longer sequences) was calculated". Scores are pairwise comparisons of spike order versus place-field-centre order among "local" spikes ("within 80 ms of its neighbor and whose place field center was separated by less than 80 cm"). "The time window was allowed to keep increasing until there were five consecutive time window steps (50 ms) without an increase in the score or the window reached 600 ms." Run four times per session (left/right laps × forward/backward) (main text Experimental Procedures, p. 702–703).

- Signal: sorted place cells ("3088 place fields from the 2183 place cells"; putative interneurons excluded, p. 702).

Gates (Supplemental, "Sequence significance testing", PDF p. 21):

- SWR power: "SWR power was calculated at each point in time by averaging the SWR power from all hippocampal tetrodes (filtered between 180 Hz and 220 Hz) (Jackson et al., 2006). Only events occurring at reward sites with a SWR power greater than 2 standard deviations above the mean for the session were considered." How power is measured (Hilbert amplitude, squared, log) is deferred to Jackson 2006; which part of a window must exceed 2 SD (peak, any sample, mean) is not stated. Smoothing: not stated.

- Cells and behaviour: "17593 events across 31 sessions were identified in which at least three cells were active, the SWR power was above threshold, and the animal was paused at reward sites." "Paused" has no speed threshold in the text; the main text says replays were analysed "while animals were paused at reward sites" (p. 703).

- Duration: the window limits, 40–600 ms, are the only duration limits.

- Merging: "Because replays were initially identified for left and right laps separately, identical pairs (i.e. replay pairs occurring over the central stem) were merged." Other merging: not stated.

- No theta or sleep criterion (awake task).

Later analysis restrictions (not detection): two bootstrap shuffles (300 each; spike-time and cell-identity), "If the unshuffled replay score was greater than 95% of both independent sets of shuffled sequences, the replay was deemed significant"; "only events with 3 or more cells were included in the analyses"; 1719 significant of 17593. Bayesian decoding (20 ms window) of the identified replay times is a control.

## Inherited from

Jackson, Johnson & Redish 2006 ([primary LFP Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6674885/), primary Methods inspected) uses across-channel mean Hilbert amplitude, log transformation and a 2.5-SD threshold. Gupta changes the band to 180–220 Hz and threshold to 2 SD; whether it retained Jackson’s log transformation remains an inference.

## Analysis and interpretation

The custom sequence detector scans in 10 ms steps using flexible 40-600 ms windows. The separate Bayesian control uses a 20 ms sliding window on the identified replay times (main Experimental Procedures, printed p. 703); its sliding step is not stated. The 10 ms sequence-scan step is not a Bayesian window or a documented Bayesian step.
