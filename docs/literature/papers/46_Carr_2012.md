## Method as implemented

DETECTION:

- Channels (Experimental Procedures, Analysis, p. 710): "SWRs were identified on the basis of peaks in the LFP recorded from tetrodes in the CA1 stratum pyramidale. CA1 stratum pyramidale tetrodes were identified using postmortem histology and the presence of at least two putative excitatory neurons." CA1 only (unlike Karlsson 2009, which also used CA3). LFP "sampled at 1.5 KHz, and digitally filtered between 0.5 Hz and 400 Hz" (p. 710).

- Filter / envelope / smoothing (p. 710): "The raw LFP data was band pass filtered between 150–250 Hz and the SWR envelope was calculated using the Hilbert transform and smoothed with a Gaussian (4 ms SD)."

- Threshold and minimum duration (p. 710): "SWR events were identified as times when the smoothed envelope exceeded 3 SD above the mean for at least 15 ms." The 15 ms applies to the above-threshold run ("that prolonged threshold crossing event").

- Combination rule (main text, Results, p. 700): "SWRs were detected by selecting periods when ripple power (150–250 Hz) on any CA1 tetrode exceeded 3 SD above the mean when animals were moving less than 4 cm/s." → any-tetrode (Karlsson rule). How overlapping per-tetrode events are joined is not restated here (inherited from Karlsson 2009: combined).

- Bounds (p. 710): "The entire SWR event was defined as including times immediately before and after that prolonged threshold crossing event during which the envelope exceeded the mean (Cheng and Frank, 2008)."

- Normalization period for the SWR envelope: not stated.

- Maximum duration: not stated. Observed: "all SWRs detected had a duration >50 ms" (p. 702) — a description, not a criterion.

- Speed (p. 710): "Analyses of awake SWRs were restricted to when the animal was moving less than 4 cm/s in either of the two W-tracks". Main text (p. 700) puts it inside the detection sentence ("... exceeded 3 SD above the mean when animals were moving less than 4 cm/s"). Which sample(s) are tested: not stated.

- Robustness (p. 700): "All results were consistent when we restricted our analyses to SWRs detected with a 5 SD threshold".

ANALYSIS restrictions:

- Quiescent SWRs (p. 710): "quiescent SWRs to times when the animal was had been immobile for at least 1 min in the rest box."

- Isolation (p. 710): "We excluded any SWRs that occurred in a 1 s window following detection of another SWR so that no SWRs occurred during the baseline period." Also (p. 702): "we restricted our analysis to the first SWR of each train."

- Candidate replay events (Decoding, p. 711): "Candidate replay events were defined as SWRs during which at least five place cells fired at least one spike each." (place cell: peak rate >= 3 Hz, SWRs excluded from rate maps).

## Inherited from

- Cheng & Frank 2008 (Neuron 57:303; Dropbox PDF → a local copy), cited for the bounds. Its "Detection of HFEs and Ripples" section: "LFP signals were recorded from one arbitrarily selected channel of each tetrode ... On a given day, the tetrode with the largest number of isolated neurons was chosen for ripple analysis. The LFP envelope was determined by Hilbert transforming the LFP signal from that tetrode after band pass filtering between 150 and 250 Hz. Events were detected if the envelope exceeded threshold for at least 15 ms. Events included times around the triggering event during which the envelope exceeded the mean. Overlapping events were combined. A high threshold of mean + 6 SD ... HFEs were detected with a lower threshold of mean + 3 SD." (single tetrode, no smoothing stated). Carr's multi-tetrode any-tetrode rule comes from Karlsson & Frank 2008/2009 (both cited as the source of the recordings).

## Code

No code link in the paper (custom Matlab + Chronux). The Frank-lab code snapshot was checked below.

### Related code

- **Lab.** [droumis/FFPhy @fce2048](https://github.com/droumis/FFPhy/tree/fce2048), a snapshot of the Frank lab's analysis code (added 2019).
  - Carr's own `Functions/riptriggeredspectrogram_MC.m` (the paper's SWR-triggered spectrograms) filters CA1 cells (line 38) and calls `getripples(..., 'minstd', 3)` (line 85).
  - `Functions/getripples.m`: `minrip = 1` (line 29) and `inripple = (nrip >= minrip)` (line 116), so an event on any one CA1 tetrode counts and overlapping events are joined.
  - Each tetrode (`DFFunctions/extractripples.m`): 4 ms smoothing (line 57) and 15 ms (line 74), applied to the run above threshold (`DFFunctions/extractevents.cpp` line 127).

- **Rule in the code, not stated as a criterion in the paper:** joined events of 50 ms or less are dropped (`getripples.m` line 135). That fits the paper's remark that "all SWRs detected had a duration >50 ms". It is not the paper's own code, so Min. Duration stays 15.

- CRCNS hc-6 (the data of Karlsson 2009 and Carr 2012) lists no ripple files.

## Uncertainties

FFPhy supports the channel-union and detection interpretation, but exact historical per-session options remain unproven.

## Package mapping

Packaged primary method: `carr_2012` in [literature_methods.py](../../../src/ripple_detection/literature_methods.py). Its docstring records implementation choices and assumptions. Simulation checks establish that it runs; they do not establish equivalence to the authors’ original event set.

The method defaults to `stage="detection"`. Use `stage="decoding_candidates"` for the analysis participation and duration restrictions described above.
