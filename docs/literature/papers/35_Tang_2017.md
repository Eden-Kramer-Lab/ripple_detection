# Tang 2017 — Hippocampal-prefrontal reactivation during learning is stronger in awake as compared to sleep states
Source: the extracted text (pdftotext of the Dropbox PDF; J Neurosci Accepted Manuscript with line numbers); title verified: yes (Tang W, Shin JD, Frank LM, Jadhav SP, J Neurosci 37:11789, doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2291-17.2017)
Trigger: SWR

## Method as implemented
DETECTION (Materials and Methods, "SWR detection and modulation", manuscript lines 226–238):
- "SWRs were detected as previously described (Cheng and Frank, 2008; Jadhav et al., 2016). Briefly, LFPs from multiple CA1 tetrodes were filtered into the ripple band (150-250 Hz) and the envelope of band-passed LFPs was determined by Hilbert transform."
- Threshold and combination: "SWR were initially detected when the envelope exceeded a threshold (mean + 3 SD) on at least one tetrode." → any-tetrode (Karlsson rule).
- Speed: "Detection of SWRs was performed only when animals' head speed was < 4 cm/s." → stated as part of detection; which samples are tested (or whether detection is masked to low-speed times) is not stated.
- Bounds: "SWR events were then defined as times around the initially detected events during which the envelope exceeded the mean."
- Smoothing: not stated. Minimum duration: not stated. Normalization period: not stated. Maximum duration: not stated. Merging of overlapping per-tetrode events: not stated (inherited: combined).
- LFP "sampled at 1.5 kHz" (line 191).

ANALYSIS restrictions:
- Sleep (line 236): "For sleep analysis, only SWRs that occurred in slow-wave sleep periods were included."
- Sleep scoring (lines 212–224, following Mizuseki 2011 and Kay 2016): awake = head speed > 4 cm/s plus times < 4 cm/s within 7 s after moving; "Candidate sleep periods were identified as times with head speed < 4 cm/s preceded by a 1 min immobility period"; SWS vs REM by the theta (6–12 Hz)/delta (1–4 Hz) power ratio averaged over CA1 tetrodes with "A threshold (mean + 1 SD) of the theta/delta ratio"; "visually inspected for accuracy".
- Isolation (lines 237–238): "For SWR-triggered rasters/PSTHs/spectral analysis, only SWRs separated from others by at least 500 ms were included."
- Replay (lines 310–312): "Candidate events were defined as SWR events during which at least 5 place cells fired" (10 ms bins).
- Theta periods (lines 288–289): "> 5 cm/s and no SWRs detected on any of the CA1 tetrodes with a 3 SD criterion".

## Inherited from
- Cheng & Frank 2008 (a local copy): single tetrode with most units, Hilbert envelope 150–250 Hz, "exceeded threshold for at least 15 ms", bounds at mean, "Overlapping events were combined"; no smoothing stated.
- Jadhav 2016 → Karlsson & Frank 2009: multiple CA1 tetrodes, 4 ms Gaussian smoothing, >= 15 ms above 3 SD on at least one tetrode, bounds at mean, overlapping events combined.
- Inference: the 15 ms minimum on the above-threshold run and 4 ms smoothing are the inherited defaults; Tang does not state them.
- Sleep scoring: Kay et al. 2016 (Dropbox PDF → a local copy), whose REM threshold was set manually (1.2–1.8, >= 10 s); Tang instead uses an automatic mean + 1 SD threshold.

Remaining deviations:
- Speed: "Detection ... performed only when head speed was < 4 cm/s" may mean detection restricted to low-speed times (event wholly in < 4 cm/s); package tests only the first and last sample (<= 4). `exclude_movement_by_majority` or a max-speed filter on the output are the alternatives; the paper does not say.
- Smoothing and minimum duration are not stated in Tang; using Karlsson's 4 ms / 15 ms is an inference from the cited lineage.
- 500 ms isolation is two-sided (drop both members of a close pair); `exclude_close_events` keeps the first of a cluster. Needs user code (analysis only).
- SWS scoring (theta/delta ratio threshold, speed history, visual inspection) is not in the package; `require_overlap` / `exclude_overlap` accept user-computed intervals.
- Negligible items as for Karlsson 2009.
