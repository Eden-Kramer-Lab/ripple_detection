## Method as implemented
Detection criteria. Sleep frames: Supplementary Materials and Methods, "Detection of frames during slow-wave sleep in the sleep box", txt lines 1273–1289. Awake frames: txt 1291–1295.

- **State gate (detection): SWS from immobility + theta/delta relative to its mean, plus manual review.** "Frames were detected during slow-wave sleep periods in the sleep box based on animal immobility (velocity below 2 cm/s, set at this value since the rat pups occasionally moved/twitched during sleep) and low theta/delta ratio on the LFP (epochs below the mean theta/delta ratio, computed on the Hilbert transform for the respective frequencies, 4-10 Hz for theta and 1-3 Hz for delta, and smoothed with a 10 s Gaussian) to exclude epochs of rapid-eye movement sleep. The non-REM sleep epochs were further reviewed manually and adjusted to ensure potential spurious detections of theta oscillation were excluded from further analysis."
  - Ratio: theta 4–10 Hz over delta 1–3 Hz, Hilbert, 10 s Gaussian. The threshold is **relative**: below the mean theta/delta ratio (the averaging period, presumably the session, is not stated). Then manual adjustment.
  - Immobility: velocity < 2 cm/s. No minimum duration stated (the Neuron paper, row 22, has >= 5 min).
  - Main text: "slow-wave sleep with continuous animal immobility (velocity <2 cm/s) at minimal theta power (see materials and methods and fig. S5)" (txt ~856–859).
- **Signal:** "the combined population activity of all pyramidal neurons in 1 ms bins was calculated and convolved with a Gaussian kernel of 15 ms." Sorted units: "Single units were isolated by a manual cluster-cutting procedure using Xclust3 ... Only well-isolated units were used" (txt 1103–1109). 15 ms as SD or width: not stated.
- **Threshold / duration / participation:** "Periods when the population activity of at least 5 distinct place-responsive cells exceeded 2 standard deviations above the mean population activity for 100-800 ms during slow-wave sleep were considered as sleep frames for sequence analysis." Bounds at the 2 SD crossings (literal reading, no extension to mean). 100–800 ms applies to the above-threshold period. Place cells: "Pyramidal neurons with a peak firing rate exceeding 1 Hz were considered as place cells" (txt 1130). Mean/SD period: "mean population activity ... during slow-wave sleep" suggests SWS, but not stated explicitly.
- **Awake frames (detection):** "Frames of activity were also detected during awake rest periods on the linear track. The criteria used for detection of frames during slow-wave sleep were applied during awake rest with the exception that animal velocity was kept at under 1 cm/s to prevent contamination from run behaviors." Main text: "defined as increases in multineuronal activity (minimum of five neurons) exceeding two standard deviations (SD) above the mean at animal velocities <1 cm/s" (txt ~234–238). Whether the theta/delta criterion also applies to awake frames is ambiguous ("the criteria ... were applied ... with the exception" suggests yes, which would be unusual for awake rest).
- Merging / close events / artifact rejection: not stated.

Analysis-only items:
- **Ripple analysis (analysis only):** "Ripple detection was performed by filtering the raw LFP in the 140-250 Hz range, during low theta-delta epochs and animal immobility (velocity less than 2 cm/s) in the sleep box. The mean ripple power in this band was computed, and periods exceeding 3 standard deviations of the mean were considered as hippocampal ripples and used for ripple characterization across development. To compute the ripple power within frames, the instantaneous ripple power was z-scored by the averaged ripple power. The maximal Z-scored ripple power in the frame was considered as its associated ripple power." (txt 1387–1396). Used for Fig. 4G ("Age-dependent increase in ripple power associated with location- and trajectory-depicting sleep frames"). Ripples do not gate frames.
- Frames binned at 20 ms for decoding (txt 1286).

Recipe (`counts`: (n_time, n_pyramidal_units) at 1 kHz; `is_place`: bool per unit):
```python
import numpy as np
from ripple_detection import get_multiunit_population_firing_rate, normalize_signal, multiunit_HSE_detector
from ripple_detection.core import extend_threshold_to_mean

# SWS (user code): ratio = |hilbert(bp(4,10))| / |hilbert(bp(1,3))|, 10 s Gaussian; sws = (ratio < ratio.mean()) & (speed < 2)
rate = get_multiunit_population_firing_rate(counts, 1000, smoothing_sigma=0.015)   # all pyramidal cells
z = normalize_signal(rate, normalization_mask=sws)
frames = np.array(extend_threshold_to_mean(z >= 2, z >= 2, time, minimum_duration=0.100)).reshape(-1, 2)
frames = frames[frames[:, 1] - frames[:, 0] <= 0.800]
# keep frames inside SWS with >= 5 distinct place cells spiking (user loop, counts[:, is_place])

# one-call approximation (bounds extended to the mean; unit count over all units passed):
c = counts.astype(float); c[~sws] = np.nan
events = multiunit_HSE_detector(time, c, speed, 1000, speed_threshold=np.inf, zscore_threshold=2.0,
                                minimum_duration=0.100, smoothing_sigma=0.015, maximum_duration=0.800,
                                minimum_active_units=5)
# awake frames: same on track rest periods with speed < 1 cm/s (a mask on counts, or exclude_overlap with speed >= 1 intervals)
```

Remaining deviations:
- Same as row 22: `multiunit_HSE_detector` extends to the mean (the paper bounds at 2 SD), the ceiling then applies to the extended event, `>=` vs "exceeded", and 15 ms SD vs width is unknown.
- `minimum_active_units` counts all units passed, while the paper counts place-responsive cells within a rate built from all pyramidal cells. Faithful counting needs a user loop over `counts[:, is_place]`, or a second pass.
- The manual SWS review/adjustment cannot be reproduced. The relative threshold (below the mean ratio) depends on the averaging period, which is not stated.
- Awake frames: the "<1 cm/s" condition is a period restriction. The package's `speed_threshold` tests only event endpoints, so a whole-event rule needs a mask or `exclude_overlap` on speed >= 1 intervals.
Smallest package addition (if C): n/a (B). As for row 22, a threshold-bounded option on the HSE detector, plus a `unit_mask` for which units count toward `minimum_active_units`, would make the event rule A.
