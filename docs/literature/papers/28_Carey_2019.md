# Carey 2019 — Reward revaluation biases hippocampal replay content away from the preferred outcome
Source: the extracted text (pdftotext of the Dropbox PDF, includes Methods and Supplementary figure legends); title matched (Carey, Tanaka & van der Meer, Nat Neurosci 22:1450–1459). Code and the paper's own candidate files checked on GitHub (see Code).
Trigger: SWR+MUA (a single joint score: geometric mean of an LFP "SWR similarity score" and a multiunit score)

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-019-0464-6) · [Field evidence](../evidence.csv) · [Source catalog](../sources.md)

## Method as implemented

Detection (Methods, "SWR event detection", PDF p. 12, unless noted):

- Overview: "we used a thresholding procedure based on spectral content of the local field potential, combined with adaptive thresholding of multiunit activity (MUA), to identify putative SWR complexes."

- LFP score (NOT a band-pass/Hilbert envelope): "for each recording session, a noise-corrected SWR Fourier spectrum was obtained by manual identification of example SWR events and subtracting a non-SWR 'noise' Fourier spectrum. The dot product between this noise-corrected frequency spectrum and the frequency spectrum of the local field potential was then obtained using a sliding 60-ms window to obtain an 'SWR similarity score'." No filter band is stated. Channel count not stated in the text (code: one channel, see Code).

- MUA score: "a multiunit activity detector was defined, starting from a spike density function obtained by convolving the spiking activity of all recorded units with a σ = 20 ms kernel. An adaptive contrast enhancement step was applied to the raw MUA to emphasize transient changes in firing." Units are sorted units ("all recorded units"; 16-tetrode arrays, sessions kept "if at least 40 cells were recorded in total", p. 11).

- Combination and threshold: "a composite SWR detector was obtained by taking the geometric mean of the noise-corrected frequency spectrum and the modified MUA. The resulting unitless quantity was thresholded at 4 to yield candidate events". The text does not say whether 4 is a z-score (the code shows it is not; see Code).

- Event bounds: not stated in the text (code: the crossings of the same threshold, 4).

- Restrictions: "which were further restricted by the following requirements: (1) animals could not be moving faster than 4 cm s−1; (2) the theta power could not be more than 2 s.d. above the mean; (3) minimum duration of 20 ms; and (4) minimum 5 units active."

- Maximum duration, merging of close events: not stated.

- Brain state: awake task sessions; "Rats appeared to be mostly awake during recording sessions, although some SWRs in the pre- and post-task rest periods probably occurred during the early sleep stages." No sleep scoring. The theta criterion is a detection criterion.

Later analysis restrictions (not detection):

- Sequenceless decoding: "Only SWR events with at least four active neurons were included." (Methods, "Decoding", p. 11).

- Sequence decoding: candidate sequences come from the decoded posterior (Pfeiffer & Foster 2013 procedure: 25 ms window, 5 ms step, ≥4 cells per window, break at >40 cm jumps), then "a minimum length (50 ms), a minimum number of neurons participating in the event (4) and overlap with a putative SWR for any nonzero amount of time" (Methods, "Sequence detection and selection", p. 11). These define sequences, not SWR candidates.

- Run epochs for tuning curves: speed "exceeded a fixed threshold (approximately 4 cm s−1)" (p. 11).

## Code

- Paper code: http://github.com/vandermeerlab/papers (folder `Carey_etal_submitted`); readme pins the vandermeerlab codebase to commit ad0bbd4d01726a436b36671c0a8b2db81476e946 and ships the candidate events used in the paper as `SWRcandidates/<session>-candidates.zip` ("place the SWR candidate event files in their corresponding data folders").

- I opened `R050-2014-03-29-candidates.mat` (MAT file created 9 Jun 2015). Its processing history and `evt.parameters`:
  - `cfg.history.mfun = {'iv','precand','restrict','restrict'}`; `precand` cfg: `threshold = 4`, `mindur = 0.02`, `minCells = []`.
  - `parameters.amSWR`: `weightby='amplitude'`, `win1=0.06`, `hiPassCutoff=100`, `fs=2000`, `csc='R050-2014-03-29-CSC03a.ncs'` (one channel), built from `SWRfreak` (manually identified SWR spectrum minus noise spectrum).
  - `parameters.amMUA`: `spkcap=2`, `kernelstd=40` (samples at 2 kHz = 20 ms), `noisefloor=4`.
  - `parameters`: `DetectorThreshold=4`, `SpeedLimit=10` (pixels/s; the paper's "4 cm s−1"), `ThetaThreshold=2`, `minCells=5`.
  - First `restrict` interval set: `TSDtoIV` on speed, `method='raw'`, `threshold=10`, `dcn='<'`, `merge_thr=0.05`, `minlen=0.05`. Second: `TSDtoIV` on theta power, `method='zscore'`, `threshold=2`, `dcn='<'`, `merge_thr=0.05`, `minlen=0.05`.
  - 1654 events; durations 0.020–0.258 s (median 0.0575 s); `nActiveCells` minimum 5.

- `precand.m` (at the pinned commit): `score = sqrt(SWR.data .* MUA.data); score = rescmean(score,0.5); aboveThreshold = score>cfg.threshold;` bounds from `diff(aboveThreshold)`; events with `tend-tstart < cfg.mindur` removed. So the threshold 4 is on the joint score rescaled to mean 0.5 (i.e. 8 × its mean), a single threshold, with the bounds at the same threshold; it is not a z-score.

- `amSWR.m`: sliding windowed FFT (`win1` = 60 ms), Fourier coefficients below `hiPassCutoff` (100 Hz) zeroed, score = dot product with the noise-corrected template, floored at 0, rescaled to mean 1.

- `vandermeerlab/restrict.m` keeps an interval only if `tstart >= iv.tstart & tend <= iv.tend` (containment in a low-speed and a low-theta interval).

- `GenCandidateEvents.m` (both the pinned commit and 82ba3fe, which the package cites) is a later (Jan 2016) wrapper: default `SWRmethod='AM'` (amSWR, the paper's method), `'HT'` = OldWizard Hilbert score as an alternative; thresholds by `TSDtoIV2` with `DetectorThreshold=1` (edges) and `DetectorThreshold2=3` on the z-scored score. Its `precand` call is commented out. `MASTER_Generate_Tmaze_Candidates.m` at the pinned commit sets `SWRmethod='HT'`, `MUAmethod='none'`, `ThetaThreshold=[]` with filename suffix `_HT1-3_noTheta`, an exploratory configuration; the shipped files have no suffix and record `precand`.

## Uncertainties

The stored configuration is from one released session. Template ripples were manually selected; reproducing the event rule does not recover the original manual choices. The lab's [DataLad release](../sources.md#dataset-catalog-checks) of the recordings stores, in each of the 24 sessions' `metadata.mat`, `SWRtimes` (50 fixed 80 ms example intervals; 150 in R050-2014-04-02) and `SWRfreqs`, whose `parameters` equal the `amSWR` configuration above; they can be passed as `example_ripples`. They were saved in July 2017, after the June 2015 candidates, so whether they are the examples behind the published candidates is not established. Later wrapper defaults do not establish the settings used for every session.
