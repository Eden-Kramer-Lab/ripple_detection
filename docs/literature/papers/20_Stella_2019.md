## Method as implemented
Detection criteria:
- Signal: tetrodes in dorsal CA1 stratum pyramidale, 32 tetrodes / 128 channels. "in four rats we recorded CA1 multiple-unit activities in 128 channels (32 tetrodes)" (Results, p. 2); "tetrodes were gradually moved into the dorsal CA1 cell layer (stratum pyramidale)" (STAR Methods, p. e1). Wide-band 0.4 Hz–5 kHz, digitized at 24 kHz (p. e2).
- State (detection-window) criterion, REM excluded by theta/delta ratio: "We first excluded rapid eye movement periods (REM). REM periods were detected based on the theta/delta ratio as described in Csicsvari et al. (1999b). To identify periods of theta activity, the theta/delta power ratio was measured in 1600 ms segments (800 ms steps in between measurement windows), using Thomson's multi-taper method." (STAR Methods "SWR Detection", p. e3). The ratio threshold is not stated.
- Detection is in the rest-box session after exploration: "We detected these SWR periods during the quiet immobility period after exploration in the absence of theta oscillatory periods" (Results, p. 2). Protocol: "exploration of the familiar circular open-field environment (40 mins), sleep/rest in rest box (diameter = 26cm, 50 mins)" (p. e1).
- Envelope, a wavelet rather than a band-pass filter: "In the remaining periods of sleep, we computed a wavelet transform (Morlet wavelet) of the local field potential. We used wavelet scales spanning a 5Hz – 500Hz range. We then calculated the power (root mean square) for each electrode in the 150Hz – 250Hz range" (p. e3).
- Normalization and combining channels: "z-scored it and took the maximum across the set of electrodes identified as being in CA1" (p. e3). The period the z-score statistics come from is not stated.
- Threshold and bounds: "Ripple events were then classified by taking peaks in the score passing 5 SD and extending the event window until the score dropped below 2 SD." (p. e3)
- Smoothing: not stated beyond the wavelet's own time resolution. Minimum or maximum duration: not stated for detection. Merging: not stated. Speed criterion: none stated for detection. Unit participation: none at detection.

Analysis restrictions (not detection):
- "Events containing less than five windows were removed from further analysis." (Analysis of Reactivation Events, p. e4). The windows are 8 ms fixed or 15 spikes each, so this means at least 40 ms (fixed windows) or at least 75 spikes (fixed-spike windows).
- "Only SWRs that met a quality threshold for the encoded probability ... and contained at least five encoded trajectory points were included" (Results, p. 2); fewer than three neighbouring window pairs removed (p. e4).
- The 5 cm/s speed filter is used only for rate maps (p. e2), not for SWR detection.

- Min. Duration (ms) = 40: the paper states no detection minimum. The 40 ms matches the ANALYSIS restriction "Events containing less than five windows were removed from further analysis" (5 × 8 ms). With the fixed-spike windows used for the main results it is 5 × 15 spikes, not a duration.
- Detection Notes = "SWR" (incomplete): leaves out the bounds at 2 SD, the maximum across CA1 electrodes, the Morlet-wavelet RMS power, and the REM exclusion by theta/delta ratio.

## Package mapping
Tier: B     Needs radiatum: n   Needs theta: y   Needs sleep scoring: y (REM exclusion by theta/delta ratio; no separate SWS scoring)
Recipe:
```python
from ripple_detection import filter_ripple_band, get_envelope, normalize_signal, exclude_overlap
from ripple_detection.core import extend_threshold_to_mean
filt = filter_ripple_band(lfp_ca1, fs, band=(150, 250))      # (n_time, n_electrodes)
z = normalize_signal(get_envelope(filt), normalization_mask=None)   # per-electrode z-score
z_max = z.max(axis=1)                                        # max across CA1 electrodes
events = extend_threshold_to_mean(z_max > 2, z_max > 5, time, minimum_duration=0.0)
events = exclude_overlap(np.array(events).reshape(-1, 2), rem_intervals)   # REM from user theta/delta
# (or restrict to non-REM beforehand with a mask; the REM intervals are user-computed)
```
Checked: this runs on simulate_session data (26 of 28 true ripples recovered in a 60 s synthetic test; a smoke test only).
Remaining deviations:
- Envelope: the Hilbert magnitude of an equiripple FIR band-pass, not the RMS of Morlet wavelet coefficients over 150–250 Hz scales. The time–frequency smoothing differs, so bounds and durations will differ somewhat.
- REM exclusion: the package has no theta/delta scoring. The user computes 1.6 s / 0.8 s-step multitaper theta/delta and chooses a threshold, which the paper does not give. They then pass the REM intervals to exclude_overlap, or leave REM out of the trace and the normalization.
- Normalization period not stated. Choose `normalization_mask` (e.g. non-REM rest).
- The mask must be built on the max-of-z trace. Karlsson_ripple_detector is close (an event on any channel), but it extends each channel's event to the mean (z = 0), not to 2 SD, and merges per-channel events.
- Strict ">" in the recipe matches "passing" and "dropped below". threshold_by_zscore uses ">=".
- extend_threshold_to_mean does not split at NaNs or time gaps. Build the trace per contiguous block, since NaN samples compare False and so end runs, but smoothing spreads NaN.
Smallest package addition (if C): n/a. To make it A: a `bound_threshold` (extension level) argument on Karlsson/Kay-style detectors, plus a max-over-channels combination. Theta/delta scoring stays user-side.
